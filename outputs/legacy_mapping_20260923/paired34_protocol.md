# 34 shared-QA control comparison protocol — frozen before reading control outcomes

Date 2026-09-23. Inputs are the 62 one-to-one exact `(image_ref, question, normalized answer)` matches with identical ordered organ boxes, intersected with the **already fixed** foreground inclusion IDs from `bbox_fg_gemma3_4b_n400.json`; this yields 34 QA. Inclusion uses no CIPE control values and is identical across all four overlapping general models. Bind identities through the source train SHA, old/new mapping SHA, image byte SHA from the new mapping, receipt SHAs, and ordered old/new sample IDs. Require same image_ref/question/answer/ordered organ boxes and old `cipe_relevant ==` new `cipe_organ` for every selected QA/model before analyzing controls. No item may be dropped based on outcome.

For each selected QA and model, define `D_L = cipe_random - cipe_relevant` (one stored legacy random mask) and `D_F = mean(cipe_control_draws) - cipe_organ` (five stored foreground-matched masks). The primary contrast is the QA-weighted mean of `D_L - D_F` over these 34 fixed QA, **conditional on the stored masks**. Report the old and new mean D separately, plus the contrast. Do not extrapolate to all 229 foreground items, to the 400 legacy general items, or to MedTrust-TV.

Resample unique `image_ref` clusters with replacement, 20,000 replicates, Python `random.Random(20260923)`. For each replicate include every QA in sampled images and take the ordinary mean over the resulting QA rows (QA weighting; cluster multiplicity honored). Compute percentile 95% CI and four-model Bonferroni 98.75% CI (2.5/97.5 and 0.625/99.375 percentiles), with no p-values. Report the number of unique images, QA counts by image, every QA's `D_L`, `D_F`, contrast, and the image-level QA mean contrast. Retain both CI families for every model regardless of sign.

Also report outcome-descriptive, **un-tested** half-tie win fractions: legacy per-QA `1[D_L>0]+0.5·1[D_L=0]`; foreground per-QA average across five stored draws of `1[draw-organ>0]+0.5·1[draw-organ=0]`; then equal weight across 34 QA. The old one-draw and new five-draw fractions have different per-QA Monte Carlo precision and are not treated as equivalent estimators. The data are not new preregistered confirmatory evidence.
